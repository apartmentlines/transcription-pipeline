A few review tips

Play a passage with its time button to hear two seconds of context before it; playback continues normally. Keyboard: Space plays/pauses; H/← rewinds 2 seconds; L/→ advances 2 seconds. Shift+H/← or Shift+L/→ seeks 10 seconds. J/↓ and K/↑ highlight the next or previous block; S starts a role selection. In selection mode, J/↓ and K/↑ adjust the range. O assigns Operator, C Caller, U Unsure, R removes labels, and X clears selection. These role shortcuts finish selection and move to the next block. Enter toggles the “I listened” checkbox when focused. Shortcuts work outside text fields; Escape leaves a text field. Mark uncertainty instead of guessing. To separate two speakers, put the text cursor where the voice changes and choose Split at cursor. Speaker changes follow your Operator/Caller assignments. Other passage breaks do not matter. To label a speaking turn, select its first block, extend the selection through its last block, then choose Operator or Caller. Operator is the answering-service side; Caller is the incoming side, including property staff who called. Assign a role to every block before completing review. Use Unsure when you cannot identify the role. Saving keeps a draft in this browser; download feedback to keep a portable copy.
